Making Inclusive Education Possible at Scale

Project Inclusion (PI), an initiative of Sri Aurobindo Society-Rupantar, works to strengthen inclusive education across India’s school systems. We focus on large-scale teacher capacity building, professional support and institutional partnerships. This enables educators, families and education systems to create learning environments where children with Neurodevelopmental Disorders (NDDs) can learn and thrive.

Our Approach

Inclusive education at scale requires coordinated action across classrooms, families, professional support systems and public institutions. Project Inclusion brings these stakeholders together through teacher capacity building, early identification, intervention support and institutional partnerships.

Advisory Committee

Prof. Dr. Sudesh Mukhopadhyay

Chairperson

Former Chairperson, Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI), and Former HoD, Inclusive Education, National Institute of Educational Planning and Administration (NIEPA). She advises government bodies, NGOs, and international agencies, including BRAC Bangladesh, UNICEF, USAID, and the World Bank.

Sh. KVS Rao

Member

A former civil servant across Central Government Ministries and Departments, including 20 years dedicated to Health and Disability. He is a life coach, conducting workshops on physical and mental well-being, and author of eight books on self-awareness, holistic living, and disability policy.

Sh. Kumar Raju

Member

With 15+ years of experience across NGOs, CSR initiatives, Ministry of Education, and UNICEF, Mr Raju is currently the Director of the Indian Sign Language Research and Training Centre (ISLRTC) Delhi.

Prof. Dr. Renu Malaviya

Member

Former HoD Education at Lady Irwin College and associated with CARE, Save the Children, UNFPA, ILO, UGC, and NCERT. She has collaborated with University of Edinburgh and King's College London on education research, and authored 25+ books on child development and inclusive education.

Ms. Manisha Verma

Member

A seasoned rehabilitation professional, RCI-registered Special Educator, specialist in Intellectual Disability. She currently serves as Faculty in Inclusive Education at FDRC Army Welfare Education Society.

Dr. Priyanka Sharma

Member

Director, Research and Assessment, Australian Council for Educational Research (ACER) India. Formerly, she was associated with the Department of Higher Education, CBSE, and Azim Premji Foundation. She has led the National Student Assessment (Bangladesh) and helped establish the Delhi Board of School Education (DBSE).

Dr. Simmi Mahajan

Member Convener

Chief Human Resources Officer, Sri Aurobindo Society-Rupantar, and Chief Program Officer, Project Inclusion. Under her leadership, Project Inclusion has reached 32 states and UTs, orienting 3.5+ lakh educators. She has received Dr. Yudhvir Singh Award and also serves on the Autism Committee of Andhra Pradesh.
